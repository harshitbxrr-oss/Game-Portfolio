06 — THE DESIGN DECISIONS

Less guesswork.
More agency.

Every design choice balances guidance with user control — and makes its trade-offs visible.

01

Visualisation-first,
not marketplace-first

START WITH THEIR WALL

Begin with the user’s space, then move to a preview before professional comparison. That lets the eventual match respond to the chosen direction.

The trade-off — People who already know what they want take a few extra steps. Direct professional discovery remains a secondary route.

02

AI guidance,
with room to choose

KEEP PEOPLE IN CONTROL

AI narrows the field using the wall, style, budget and property type — while manual browsing, editable preferences, saved ideas and custom concepts remain available.

The trade-off — The interface must explain its recommendations without making alternatives harder to find.

03

From lead
to completion

MAKE THE COST VISIBLE

Define a structured journey for estimates, quote comparison, scheduling and payment protection, rather than handing users off after discovery.

The trade-off — Protected payment adds real operational complexity around disputes, cancellations, scope changes and payout release.
